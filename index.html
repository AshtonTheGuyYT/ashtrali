notes

entries are versioned

every entry carries a status — stable, updated, or legacy. when a provider's guardrails change, the maintainer who owns the entry re-tests and updates the flag.

attribution is preserved

each prompt lists the maintainer who wrote it. fork them, adapt them — but keep the credit intact when you redistribute.

read before you use

published for research and prompt-craft study. you are responsible for what you do with them and for complying with the terms of any model provider you run them against.

about

ashtrali is an independent archive maintained by two people. no submissions, no ads, no telemetry. entries are plaintext and intended to be copied out.
